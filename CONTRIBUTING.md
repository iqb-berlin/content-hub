# Contributing

## AI-assisted development

ContentPool has been developed predominantly with AI assistance, as reported by
its maintainer. This describes the development process, not a determination of
copyright ownership or proof that all generated output is original. Historical
tool and model details have not been reconstructed or certified.

AI-assisted contributions are welcome. For substantial generation or changes,
record the tool and model/version (if known), the affected areas, and the checks
actually performed in the pull request description. Do not invent unavailable
provenance details or claim human review that has not taken place. An optional
`Assisted-by: <tool/model>` commit trailer can supplement that description.

Contributors must understand and test their submissions and identify any known
third-party code, sources, licenses and required notices. Human maintainers review
contributions and unresolved provenance findings before merging. Automated scan
success is not legal approval. Do not ask an assistant to reproduce a specific
third-party implementation without checking the permission to use it.

## License and provenance

The project uses the unmodified Apache License 2.0 for rights that its contributors
hold and can license. This does not create copyright in otherwise unprotected
material or relicense third-party components. Keep applicable third-party license
and attribution notices. The Apache-2.0 package metadata check only checks the
consistency of declarations, not ownership or license compatibility.

See [compliance reports](docs/compliance.md) for SCANOSS, ScanCode, dependency
inventories and their limitations. The SCANOSS workflow sends code fingerprints
and file metadata to the public OSSKB service; it does not upload raw source text.
Do not include secrets or confidential third-party material in contributions.
